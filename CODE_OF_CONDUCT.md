# Code of Conduct

The Singulisp project's mission is to write fast code and to build a language that makes code run
as fast as possible.

Project spaces exist for the technical work required by that mission. Discussion of the language
specification, implementation, standard library, tools, documentation, and verification methods
needed to make machines run correctly, predictably, and as fast as possible is in scope. Unrelated
topics are not.

## Humans and Machines

**AI writes. Machines judge.**<br>
**Humans take full responsibility.**

The project's implementation is generated by AI. Humans contribute the problems to solve,
requirements, constraints, counterexamples, reproducible bugs, and measurement results. Machines
evaluate types, ownership, effects, correctness, generated code, and execution speed against stated
requirements and measurement conditions. Maintainers determine the project's scope and priorities,
decide whether to adopt AI output, and are responsible for the artifacts they adopt.

Review focuses on generated code, designs, measurement results, and documentation. Personal
characteristics, titles, backgrounds, and identities do not constitute technical evidence.

This does not ask participants to leave their humanity at the door. It asks that who someone is not
be treated as evidence for a technical claim.

## Technical Discussion

Rigorous criticism and thorough verification of designs, code, measurement results, and performance
claims are welcome. Do not hesitate to disagree. Criticism must remain directed at technical
content.

Agreement is not required. Comments must be technically relevant.

Wherever possible, support performance claims with reproducible code, measurements, output, or
concrete reasoning. Incorrect claims will be corrected regardless of who made them.

## Out-of-Scope Topics and Conduct

The following are out of scope in project spaces:

- Topics unrelated to the design, implementation, use, or maintenance of Singulisp
- Debates unrelated to Singulisp concerning politics, social movements, ideologies, beliefs, or
  other general controversies
- Discussion of participants' personal characteristics or private lives, and speculation about
  motives they have not stated
- Advertising, solicitation, or spam
- Repeatedly reintroducing resolved matters or topics ruled out of scope without new grounds

Personal attacks, insults, threats, harassment, impersonation, and disclosure of private
information are also prohibited. Do not turn criticism of technical artifacts into attacks on
their creators.

## Moderation

Maintainers may hide, remove, or lock posts that interfere with technical work and may restrict
participation when necessary. They are not required to continue discussions they have determined
to be out of scope or to repeatedly debate that determination.

The criteria are whether the content is necessary to the work of making Singulisp run correctly,
predictably, and as fast as possible, and whether the conduct surrounding the post complies with
this Code.

Reports of conduct prohibited by this Code are not considered out of scope.

Use GitHub's reporting tools to report problematic content. Concerns about an individual's conduct
must not be raised in public Issues.

This Code applies to the repository, Issues, Discussions, and any other spaces managed by the
project.
